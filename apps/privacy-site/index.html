Barber AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 12, 2026

Barber AI provides camera-based augmented reality try-on experiences for hair and grooming concepts. This Privacy Policy explains how Barber AI handles information when you use the app.

Information We Use

Barber AI may use your device camera to power live augmented reality effects, including Camera Kit experiences. Camera access is used to render effects on your device and to make the try-on experience work.

Camera Data

Camera frames may be processed to detect and render AR effects such as facial or hair-related overlays. Barber AI does not sell camera data. We do not use camera frames to identify you personally.

Snap Camera Kit

Barber AI uses Snap Camera Kit to provide AR Lens experiences. Snap may process technical information required to deliver Camera Kit features. Snap's own privacy terms may also apply to Camera Kit functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited technical information with service providers only as needed to operate the app, provide AR effects, maintain reliability, or comply with legal obligations.

Children

Barber AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
